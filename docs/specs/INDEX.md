# lesko-business-landingpage
Status: as-built 2026-09-30

The business landing page at leskobusiness.com: static pages on a Cloudflare
Worker. The sales page's three buttons go to our own one-step `/checkout`,
whose card form `worker.js` turns into a Recurly purchase (`/api/subscribe`),
then `/welcome`. It owns those pages and the Worker routes; Recurly itself,
provisioning, email and the checkout analytics live in lesko-provisioning,
lesko-checkout and kit-pipeline.
