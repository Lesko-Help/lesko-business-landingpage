How it works

Three steps from "is this even real?" to your first funded application.

No 12-week course. No secret system. Just a clear roadmap with a guide, a community, group coaching, resources, and AI tools that do the heavy lookup work for you.

Step One
01 · Get your call sheet

Generate your personalized report

Tell our AI your situation: your work, your location, your goals. It produces a curated “call sheet” of grants you actually qualify for, drawn from Matthew's vetted database. Most members get theirs in under 5 minutes.

Why this works

There are three ways to get your personal call sheet, and you can make as many as you want:

  • Generate one with the Lesko AI researcher
  • Ask the team to build one for you in the Questions Channel
  • Join a call sheet class and we create it together

It becomes your baseline report: a highly focused, highly specialized list that kickstarts your grant search.

Common questions

Things people ask before joining.

Are these actually real grants? It feels too good. +

Yes. Every program in our database is a real, government or foundation-administered grant. Matthew has been cataloging them for 50 years. The catch isn't that they don't exist. It's that they're scattered across thousands of agencies, foundations, and state programs that don't market themselves. Our job is to surface the ones you qualify for.

I'm a freelancer or a one-person business. Do grants even apply to me? +

More than you'd expect. There are programs specifically for sole proprietors, individual artists, freelance researchers, women-owned businesses, veteran-owned businesses, rural entrepreneurs, and many other niches. The AI report is built to find the ones that match your specific situation, not just generic small-business programs.

How is this different from just searching Grants.gov myself? +

Grants.gov is one source: the federal one. Most of the funding for small operators actually comes from state agencies, regional economic development offices, and private foundations that aren't on Grants.gov at all. Our database covers all of those, and the coaching helps you actually navigate the application, not just find it.

Will someone write my grant application for me? +

No, and that's intentional. Grant writers cost $2,000–$10,000+ per application and can't fund themselves working on a freelance budget. We coach you through writing your own, with feedback at every step. Most members find that after one or two applications, they can do it themselves with confidence.

What if I'm too busy to attend live classes? +

Classes run every weekday across multiple time zones, and most are recorded. Many members never attend live. They post in the Questions Channel, where the Lesko AI answers right away and the expert coaches follow up. The community works asynchronously too.

Is this U.S. only? +

Yes — LeskoHelp Pro is U.S. only. The database covers federal, state, local, and U.S. foundation programs, and they're available to everyone in America. Some programs don't even require you to be a citizen.

What's the refund policy? +

30-day money-back guarantee on all plans. If you join, attend a few classes, generate a report, and decide it's not for you, we refund you. No forms, no friction.
